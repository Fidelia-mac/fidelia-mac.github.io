Fidelia

Fidelia is an elegant and powerful music player created exclusively for macOS. It delivers true listening experiences with maximum sonic fidelity, preserving the quality of your library across formats through the Fidelia Audio System.

Timeless Elegance

A refined interface focused on utility. No distractions, no ads, no algorithms—just your music, organized and played back with precision.

Mastering-Grade Processing

Rebuilt DSP engine with psychoacoustic dither, frequency-dependent headphone crossfeed, and dual-engine sample rate conversion. Pre/post-effects metering, post-fader metering, and effect presets elevate every track.

Gapless and Pristine Playback

Truly gapless playback, silent scrub, drag/drop autoplay, and support for DSD and APE imports to Apple Lossless without dynamic range loss. Handles external interfaces seamlessly.

Robust Library Management

Self-healing for external volumes, batch artwork lookup, format conversion with dither, Music.app sync, playlist sort columns, and expanded metadata support. Your library stays intact and accessible.

It's Your Library

Not a store or streaming service. Fidelia is built for those who know their music and demand superior quality from hard drive to speakers.

How It Works

1. Build Your Library

Import and organize tracks effortlessly. Fidelia supports all formats, with automatic metadata handling and self-healing for external drives.

2. Fine-Tune Audio

Access mastering-grade DSP tools: crossfeed, dither, sample rate conversion, and effects. Apply presets or customize for your setup.

3. Play with Precision

Enjoy gapless playback, silent scrubbing, and metering. Drag to autoplay or sync with Music.app for seamless control.

4. Listen Purely

Optimized output to your hardware—speakers, headphones, or interfaces—delivering uncluttered, high-fidelity sound.
